# 🗺️ Sentinel HealthOS Roadmap

> Building trusted Artificial Intelligence for predictive public health through Earth Observation, explainable AI, and responsible innovation.

---

# Vision

Sentinel HealthOS is the flagship platform of **6.0 Industries**.

Our mission is to help governments, healthcare systems, researchers, and humanitarian organizations anticipate, prepare for, and respond to public health risks before they become emergencies.

We are building an AI-powered platform that combines satellite Earth Observation, climate intelligence, epidemiology, and ethical AI to improve public health decision-making.

---

# Phase 1 – Sentinel HealthOS MVP (2026)

### Objective

Develop a functional Minimum Viable Product demonstrating AI-powered public health risk prediction.

### Deliverables

- ✅ Interactive South African dashboard
- ✅ Provincial health risk map
- ✅ Weather and climate data integration
- ✅ Earth Observation imagery
- ✅ Disease surveillance layer
- ✅ Air quality monitoring
- ✅ Population density analysis
- ✅ Health facility mapping
- ✅ Explainable AI predictions
- ✅ Public health risk scoring

### Success Metrics

- Working MVP
- Pilot-ready platform
- GitHub open-source community
- Initial academic and industry collaborations
